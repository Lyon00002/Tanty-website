## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router), React, TypeScript strict, Tailwind CSS, CSS custom properties for tokens, GSAP + ScrollTrigger for advanced motion, Lucide React for functional icons, SVG for brand illustration, borders and patterns. Pinned by the client brief (2026-10-20). No backend, no database.

## Users

- Cameroonian families, mostly mothers and caregivers, choosing an infant porridge (bouillie de soja) or a family product (custard powder, snacks, peanut oil, breadcrumbs, table pepper) for daily meals. Mobile-first, often on mid-range Android phones and variable connections.
- Children are the emotional audience of the brand, not the buyers.
- Retailers, distributors and institutional partners checking that the company is credible and reachable.

## Product Purpose

The new tanty.cm is first a company showcase: who TANTY is, what it makes, why families can trust it. It includes a product catalogue (store tab) with category navigation and product pages. The catalogue has no checkout: each product page offers "Commander sur WhatsApp" with a pre-filled message. Success: a visitor understands the brand and range in seconds and reaches an order conversation or a contact.

## Positioning

TANTY is a Cameroonian brand (NTFoods SARL, Yaoundé) making soy-based infant porridge and everyday family foods since 2000. Its proof is local: local ingredients (maize, soy, peanut), local production, a range built around the Cameroonian breakfast and kitchen. The name reads as "Tanty", the affectionate word for an aunt in Cameroon.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Product photographs: the client will supply official photos later. Until then every product image is a branded placeholder. No fake packaging, no simulated photos, no stock photos, no AI-generated images.
- Prices: archived 2025 prices exist but are not confirmed; the data model has a price field left empty until the client confirms.
- Contact form: no receiving system yet; the form must say so clearly or route to email/WhatsApp.
- Do not invent certifications, awards, figures, history or claims. Unverified facts are labelled as hypotheses in data, never displayed as facts.
- Brand colors are measured approximations from compressed files (see INVENTAIRE.md section 4), not published values.

## Brand Commitments

- Name and logo: wordmark "TANTY" in dark green with a small Africa globe above the A, a red elliptical ribbon around the word, "Since 2000". No vector file exists; the logo must be redrawn faithfully, not redesigned.
- Measured colors: yellow #FECC00, green #1B6430 (dark #054A1A), ribbon red #E42A28.
- Known slogans on packaging: "Pour mieux grandir !" / "For a better growth!".
- Bilingual packaging (French / English). Site language: French first.
- Mascots, illustrations, patterns and borders are new, original brand assets created for this site (hand-authored SVG, no AI imagery).

## Product Principles

1. Warm and joyful for children, credible for parents: play in the details, clarity in the information.
2. Truth before decoration: every claim on the site is sourced or absent.
3. The brand universe (mascot, borders, patterns) is a system reused across web, packaging and social, not page decoration.
4. Mobile and low bandwidth first: light SVG, no heavy media by default.
5. Products are the heroes of the catalogue; placeholders must hold their place gracefully until real photos arrive.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced-motion support, decorative SVG hidden from assistive tech, readable body text on small Android screens.
